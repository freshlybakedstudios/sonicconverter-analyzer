What each platform will do to it

PlatformNormalizes toGain appliedMeaning

Platform targets are the published defaults (Spotify −14, Apple Music −16, YouTube −14, Amazon −14, Tidal −14, Deezer −15 LUFS). A negative gain means the platform turns your master down; you paid for loudness nobody hears. Spotify does not turn quiet masters up past their true peak, so a quiet, peaky master stays quiet.

How it measures

Whole file, both channels, ITU-R BS.1770-4 K-weighting with gating (integrated). Short-term is a 3-second window, loudness range is the 95th minus 10th percentile of gated short-term blocks, true peak is measured at 4× oversampling. This is the same standard the streaming platforms use for normalization, so the numbers you see here are the numbers they see.
